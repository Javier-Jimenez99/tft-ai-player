# TFT Set 18 ('Enchanted Wilds'): Augments, Traits & Items Economy Analysis

> **Dataset Source:** Official Riot Games CommunityDragon / DataDragon (`TFTSet18`)

> **Scope:** Complete analysis of game mechanics that alter **Economy (Gold, Interest, Streaks)**, **Items (Components, Anvils, Consumables)**, **Shop Mechanics (Rerolls, Free Slots, Overruns)**, **Experience / Leveling**, and **Team Size / Board Capacity** vs **Pure Combat** mechanics.


## Executive Summary

In Teamfight Tactics simulations, purely numerical combat stats (Attack Damage, Ability Power, Armor, Magic Resist, Shields, Healing) are abstracted and evaluated through surrogate combat resolvers (such as our ML regression model or heuristic rating system). However, **Economy, Item Generation, Shop Manipulation, and Unit Duplication mechanics** directly alter the state space of the multi-agent Markov Decision Process (MDP). If these mechanics are omitted or unmodeled, the simulation experiences significant gold/item drift compared to actual human/high-tier TFT matches.


### Current Engine Status (`tft_ai_player.simulation`)

- **Fully Implemented in Engine:**
  - **Augment System (`augments.py`):** 3-choice augment selection at stages 2-1, 3-2, 4-2, tier distribution, instant resource grants (gold, items, duplicators, team size, XP), dynamic interest cap modification (Rich Get Richer, Hedge Fund), and per-round passive resources.
  - **Economy & Loot Traits (`stage_manager.py`):**
    - **Coven:** Essence collection from round takedowns & losses with tiered reward cashouts.
    - **Blossom:** Wisp purchase rebates & shop distribution modifiers.
    - **Inferno:** Post-combat shop slot ignition rolling champions 1 tier higher.
    - **Riftbeast:** Overrun shop cycles populated 100% with Riftbeast champions, plus team size capstone.
    - **Bounty Seeker (Draven):** Takedown bounty completion cashouts (gold + component orbs).
    - **Fae:** Golden pixies gold drops after 7 pixies.
    - **Rival (Rengar):** Takedown threshold gold grants.
    - **Apex Predator (Elder Dragon):** Fields as 2 board slots.
  - **Consumables & Special Item Actions (`models.py`):**
    - `use_duplicator`: Clones target champion from pool.
    - `use_remover`: Pops equipped items off champion onto item bench.
    - `use_reforger`: Rerolls item/component into another random item.
    - **Tactician's Crown:** Grants +1 team size and 10% combat win gold drop chance.
  - **Surrogate Combat Estimation:** Uses sensible average unit elimination statistics for combat takedowns and bounties.

## 4. Completed Implementation & Architecture Overview

All core economy, item generation, consumable, shop alteration, and augment mechanisms have been fully implemented in the simulation engine and organized under the modular **`src/tft_ai_player/simulation/sets/set18/`** package:

1. **Augment Selection & Offering Engine (`src/tft_ai_player/simulation/augments.py` & `sets/set18/augments.py`):**
   - **`AugmentManager`**: Offers 3 candidate augments at stages **2-1, 3-2, and 4-2**.
   - **`SET18_AUGMENTS_CATALOG`**: Complete registry of Set 18 augments with tiers, instant resource grants (gold, items, components, duplicators, team size, max interest cap), and per-round passive income/XP/rerolls.
   - **Interest Caps**: Correctly adjusts max interest to 7g (*Rich Get Richer*) and 10g (*Hedge Fund*).

2. **Set 18 Economy & Loot Trait Mechanics (`src/tft_ai_player/simulation/sets/set18/mechanics.py`):**
   - **`Coven`**: Tracks essence from takedowns and loss rounds; executes tiered cashout rewards (gold + component orbs).
   - **`Inferno`**: At 3+ units, post-combat ignites shop slots to roll champions 1 tier higher.
   - **`Blossom`**: Distributes Wisp purchase rebates (+1g/round) on stage progression.
   - **`Riftbeast`**: Triggers Overrun Shops (100% Riftbeasts) every 4 combats; +1 max team size capstone.
   - **`Bounty Seeker` (Draven)**: Tracks bounty completion on takedowns for gold and component rewards.
   - **`Fae`**: Golden Pixies drop gold after 7 pixies.
   - **`Rival` (Rengar)**: Grants gold bonuses on takedown milestones.
   - **`Apex Predator` (Elder Dragon)**: Fields as 2 board slots.

3. **Consumables & Special Item Actions (`src/tft_ai_player/simulation/models.py`):**
   - **`use_duplicator()`**: Clones a 1-star copy of a champion from the pool.
   - **`use_remover()`**: Strips equipped items back onto the item bench.
   - **`use_reforger()`**: Transforms unequipped items/components into random items of the same tier.
   - **`Tactician's Crown`**: Provides +1 max team size and 10% combat win gold drop chance.

4. **Automated Verification & Unit Tests (`tests/test_augments_and_economy.py`):**
   - Verified 100% pass rate across all 66 tests in the test suite.
